I am a PhD student at the [Université de Lausanne](https://www.unil.ch/), in the Section de langues et civilisations slaves et de l'Asie du Sud, working under the supervision of Prof. Ingo Strauch. My dissertation, *The Buddha's Journey to the West — Barlaam and Josaphat in the Eurasian Linguistic Context*, traces how a Buddhist life-of-the-Buddha narrative travelled across Sanskrit, Middle Persian, Arabic, Georgian, Greek, and Latin, and what its many translations reveal about contact between the linguistic traditions of Eurasia.

My broader research sits at the intersection of historical linguistics, philology, and digital humanities. I work on Buddhist, Christian, and Islamic textual traditions of Central Asia and the Caucasus; on translations of Greek and Indian sciences into Arabic and Syriac; and on computational tools (NLP, OCR, HTR) for ancient and medieval languages.

Before Lausanne I read Indo-European Linguistics and Semitic Philology at Friedrich-Schiller-Universität Jena, and Classical Philology and South Asian Studies at Heidelberg.
